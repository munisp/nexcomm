# Compliance Source Notes

- The AICPA describes the Trust Services Criteria as control criteria for evaluating and reporting controls over **security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy** of systems used to provide products or services. Source: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022
- The European Commission describes GDPR principles as **lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, accuracy, integrity and confidentiality, and accountability**. It also identifies data-subject rights including information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and automated decision-making/profiling-related rights. Source: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/data-protection-explained_en
- These sources are control anchors, not a legal determination. The assurance prompt requires qualified legal/compliance review to determine applicability, jurisdiction-specific retention, and formal evidence requirements.

- The SEC’s broker-dealer electronic recordkeeping guide describes a Rule 17a-4 audit-trail alternative that must preserve a complete time-stamped trail of modifications and deletions, action date/time, identity where applicable, and information needed to maintain security, signatures, authenticity, reliability, and recreation of the original record. This applies only to entities within that rule’s scope. Source: https://www.sec.gov/investment/amendments-electronic-recordkeeping-requirements-broker-dealers
- The FFIEC IT Examination Handbook provides examination material covering audit, business continuity management, development/acquisition/maintenance, information security, outsourcing, and payment systems. It is a financial-institution control reference, not a universal legal standard. Source: https://ithandbook.ffiec.gov/
